# Daymark product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals planning study, work and personal projects at a desk in daylight, with quick mobile checks between activities. Their primary task is choosing the next useful action and keeping commitments visible.

## Product Purpose

A private personal productivity workspace for creating, organizing and completing tasks. Success means reliable deadlines, clear progress and frictionless editing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, encouraging. Use direct language and honest progress.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard clutter, oversized metric tiles and unfinished generic CRUD screens.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep commitments and progress grounded in real data.
- Preserve context when editing.
- Make private ownership and failure recovery dependable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, semantic navigation, reduced motion and textual status indicators.
